#### About me

I'm an AVP Data Engineer at NatWest in Bengaluru, working on the infrastructure behind LLM applications: RAG pipelines, NL-to-SQL engines, agentic AI and LLMOps. Before that I spent ten years building event-driven data platforms on AWS (Kafka, Lambda, Glue, Redshift), including three and a half years in Edinburgh. The platform I work on carries 500M+ transactions a day at sub-5-second latency. Outside work I build and open-source tools, and I talk about what I learn.
